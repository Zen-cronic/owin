ZDR

Nemotron reads.
Plain code decides.

  1. Tavily fetches each vendor page whole. With its fetch time, hash and “Last updated” date, so Stale is a dated observation, not a hunch.
  2. NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Super reads it three times. The open-weight model on Nebius Token Factory turns the page into fields with one quote each, and every quote is checked against the page word for word.
  3. Plain code decides. An answer is filled only when every successful reading agrees, at least two of them; otherwise it goes to a person. A test feeds model prose into every stamp, and nothing moves.
  4. Your buyer gets a read-only pack. PDF, CSV or link, with the vendor’s sentence, link and fetch time behind every answer, and none of your internal notes.

It answers when its readings agree.
When they don’t, it asks you.

What we’ll tell you
before you find it

  • The demo company is fictional. Lumen Notes, its endpoints and its ZDR settings are invented, and Owin cannot see vendor consoles. Every vendor sentence, link and fetch time is real.
  • One labeller, no human audit yet. An AI coding agent labelled every item and a second pass spot-checked. The one confident miss is a label error we found ourselves and kept, because the labels were pre-registered.
  • The split is small. 11 features, so one item moves the score by 9 points.
  • The daily re-fetch is written, not scheduled. Pages are re-fetched by command, or from the app’s “Re-check this page” when the server has a key.
  • Owin is not legal advice. It quotes vendors and compares answers; it does not tell you what you are permitted to do.
